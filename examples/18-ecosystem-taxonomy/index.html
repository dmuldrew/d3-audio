🎧 How to Interpret the Audiovisual Results

  • 🌲 Depth-First Search (DFS) ➔ Coherent Biological Food Chains

    Rather than jumping haphazardly across horizontal tiers, Depth-First Search (DFS) follows a single biological food chain from primary producer up through herbivores and carnivores to apex predators and decomposers before backtracking. The listener hears energy ascend from deep ambient roots to sharp resonant leads and resolving decomposer clicks as a cohesive musical phrase.

  • 🌿 Trophic Pyramid Tier ➔ Synthesis Instrument & Octave

    Primary Producers (plants, phytoplankton) emit warm ambient drone foundations; Herbivores pluck gentle acoustic kalimba notes; Carnivores cut through with resonant synth leads; Apex Predators command deep sub-bass horns; Decomposers produce rhythmic percussive clicks.

  • ⚠️ IUCN Conservation Status ➔ Acoustic Tension (`scaleTension`)

    Healthy species categorized as Least Concern trigger pure, consonant major triads. As ecological endangerment escalates toward Vulnerable, Endangered, and Critically Endangered, scaleTension injects sharp semitone clashes, minor seconds, and unstable diminished tritones.

  • 🌎 Biome Selection ➔ Modal Landscape

    Switching biomes reshapes the acoustic ecology: Rainforest employs lush Dorian tonalities, Coral Reefs float in airy Lydian modes, Savannas resonate in earthy Pentatonic scales, and the Arctic Tundra adopts sparse Japanese Hirajoshi intervals.
