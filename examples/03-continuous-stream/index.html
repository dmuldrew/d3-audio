🎧 How to Interpret the Audiovisual Results

〰️ Unbroken Microtonal Glissando

Unlike discrete musical scales that jump in stepped semitones, this continuous oscillator glides seamlessly through decimal Hertz ($80.0\text{ Hz} \leftrightarrow 1,200.0\text{ Hz}$). Every micro-variation in elevation is audible without boundary artifacts.

🎛️ Continuous Resonant Filter Sweeps

A 24dB/octave resonant lowpass filter sweeps continuously between $200\text{ Hz}$ (deep submerged warmth) and $14,000\text{ Hz}$ (high-frequency luminescence). Filter sweep width conveys physical depth and medium density.

🧭 Spatial Vector Stereo Panning

Stereo panning glides between $-1.0$ (Full Left) and $+1.0$ (Full Right) in real time. In Theremin and Laser Scan modes, your auditory space tracks the physical lateral position across the screen with sub-millisecond accuracy.

⚡ Instantaneous Derivative ($\frac{dy}{dt}$) Modulation

Rapid rate-of-change (spikes, fault shifts, sudden gradient reversals) directly modulates the FM harmonicity and vibrato depth. Smooth trends sound pure, while volatile shocks sound buzzy and energized.
