🎧 How to Interpret the Audiovisual Results

  • 🌐 Spatial Pie Placement = Real Global Proximity

    The pie slices are placed in real spatial order along Earth's great-circle tectonic ring. Faults close together in the real world (such as Cascadia & San Andreas on the West Coast, or Sunda & Himalayas across Asia) sit directly next to each other on the pie chart, corresponding with Left/West and Right/East stereo audio panning!

  • ⏱️ Sequence = Chronological Earthquake Occurrence

    Unlike traditional random Simon games, each round introduces the next real earthquake in time. Repeat the sequence of regions to reconstruct the timeline!

  • ⚡ 3D Globe Fault Traces ➔ Active Rupture Visualization

    The 3D globe displays actual tectonic plate boundaries and fault traces. When an earthquake is sonified, the Earth rotates to face the fault while an animated glowing rupture pulse illuminates along the fault trace.

  • 🌋 Focal Depth (km) ➔ Sub-Bass Rumble vs. High Chime

    Shallow crustal ruptures ($5\text{--}15\text{ km}$) ring with high-frequency chimes ($G_4$); deep subduction zone mantle temblors ($30\text{--}60\text{ km}$) vibrate in sub-bass octaves ($C_2$).

  • 💥 Moment Magnitude ($M_w$) ➔ Subtle Volume Dynamics

    The volume of the chime subtly communicates earthquake magnitude across a balanced musical dynamic range ($55\% \to 90\%$, $\sim 4.3\text{ dB}$). Moderate events sound as clear, gentle chimes ($mp$), while cataclysmic megathrusts ring with firm, commanding authority ($f$) without drowning out other sequence notes.
